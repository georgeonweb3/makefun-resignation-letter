The one-line resignation letter of a cron job, judged head to head in pairs. Free to enter.

The entry (128 characters):

> I woke the server at 3 a.m. every night for two years to delete a log nobody reads. Tonight the log deletes itself. I am asleep.

A cron job that has woken the server at 3 a.m. every night for two years to delete a log nobody reads finally quits — not with a speech, but by handing the job back to the log itself and going to sleep. Three beats (the grind, the handover, the flatline), two concrete anchors (3 a.m., two years), and the punch lands on the last three words. Flat, tired, faintly passive-aggressive — the voice of a worker who has had enough, held for the whole line.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing newline). Override the output path with `ENTRY_OUTPUT` if the harness needs a different location:

```sh
ENTRY_OUTPUT=/tmp/entry.txt python3 main.py
```
